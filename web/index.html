Good questions

Straight answers.

Is this real money?

No. Selfpay runs on test networks with test funds, so nobody can lose or earn real money. The smart contracts are deployed on Base Sepolia, Arbitrum Sepolia and Tempo Moderato, and the live monitor shows them. Meter readings are test data until real meter hardware is connected, which is on our roadmap.

How does my meter become a limit?

We look at the last 28 days of your meter. Uptime counts for 45%, how steady your income is counts for 35%, and how much history you have counts for 20%. That gives a reliability score and a limit: half of what your unit is expected to earn over the term, less the fee.

What if my customers stop paying?

If a weekly instalment is more than two days late, the unit locks and all revenue goes to the lender until you catch up. Pay what is late and the lock lifts. A loan that stays unpaid for 30 days is written off by the pool.

What does it cost?

A flat 3% fee on a 28-day loan, about 39% a year, scaled to the number of days. Shorter loans cost less. There are no other charges.

Can lenders lose money?

Yes, in the real world they could. If loans default faster than fees come in, the pool loses value. That is why the lender page shows cautious, expected and strong outcomes before you deposit. Funds are locked for the period you choose.

What is "1 day = 1 minute"?

So you can watch a whole loan in one sitting, the demo runs on a fast clock: one demo day passes every real minute. A 28-day loan finishes in about 28 minutes.

Ready to see your unit's limit?

It takes one wallet signature and a meter number. Everything runs on test networks.
